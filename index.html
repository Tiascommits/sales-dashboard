WO (work order)
One counted sale from the Odoo export. Only work orders that pass the reporting rules are counted.
Total WO value (revenue won)
OTC plus SaaS. MRR is never added on top.
OTC
One-time revenue: devices and other one-time charges.
SaaS
Software plan revenue. API packages are included here, not counted again.
Sales channel
How the sale came in: SME, Partner, Distribution, Retention, Enterprise and so on. This is the first filter in the bar, and the KAM list narrows to KAMs who sold in it.
Partner
A company that brings or resells Tipsoi to its own clients. The Partners tab uses the ITL Partner Classification list (type, tier, KAM owner) and matches Odoo orders to a partner by name or lead source.
Segment and sub-segment
The industry of the customer, for example Manufacturing & Industrial and then RMG. It comes from the Work Order Tracker and Tipsoi’s segment list, matched by company name. See the Segments tab.
Hardware
Devices, by model (Fastface, Prompt, Tipsoi). Counted as one-time (OTC) revenue.
Software
Subscription plans, by package (Basic, Essential, Intermediate, Advance, Premium, API and so on). Counted as SaaS revenue.
MRR
Monthly recurring revenue. Shown on its own because it is a recurring figure, not part of Total WO value.
KAM
Key account manager: the salesperson a work order is credited to.
Target achievement
Total WO value divided by the assigned target.
Pace
How far through the month we are. At 17% of the month, a KAM on pace has reached about 17% of target.
MTD / MoM
Month to date (a month still in progress) and month on month (compared with the previous month).
L and Cr
L is lakh (1,00,000). Cr is crore (1,00,00,000). Example: ৳2.50 L is ৳250,000.
